# Evidence index

Results added since the October report (28 Oct, 4 Nov and 11 Nov requests and the hand-labelled action classes) that carry the conclusions. Every number is recomputed from the copies in `evidence/data/` by `python evidence/rebuild.py` (result in `checks.csv`), which also redraws the figures (`figures/`). 'Ordinary variation' = the larger of the within-group SD and the 95th percentile of differences between copies of one crystal; an effect counts only above it.
