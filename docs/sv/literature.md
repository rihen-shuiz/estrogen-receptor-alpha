# SV08 - Literature: is only H12 different between ERα LBD structures?

Step SV08 of docs/sv/RUN_SV1028.md. Written 2026-11-08.

**How this was made.** WebSearch to find candidate sources, WebFetch to open each one. Every claim below comes
from text that was opened in this session; the "source" column says what was opened (publisher/PMC full text,
preprint full text, abstract record, or a PDB entry page). WebFetch returns the opened page through a reader
model that caps verbatim quotes at 125 characters, so claims are recorded as short quotes plus close paraphrase
of the opened text, never from the search snippets.

**Rule 1.9.** Claims framed by ligand action class (agonist / antagonist / SERM / SERD) were skipped. Where an
opened source labels a *conformation* with those words, the claim is reworded to the structural state (H12 docked
against H3/H11 in the AF-2 cleft, H12 in the coactivator groove) and the ligand label is not carried over.
Ligands are named by chemistry (bulky tag, one methyl group more, >13 Å long, basic amine) only.

**Stopping.** Each question was stopped when further sources repeated what was already recorded: Q2 and Q3 reached
that point (five and seven opened sources, consistent); Q1 is answered for H3, H7, H11, H524/L525 and the dimer
interface; Q4 rests mainly on one structure paper plus two supporting sources (see "Gaps").

## Short answer to the four questions

1. **No - H12 is not the only region reported to differ.** Opened sources report, between ERα LBD crystal
   structures: helix 11 shifts with ligand chemistry (down to a single methyl group) [#3]; His524 and Leu525
   rotamers defining three structure classes across 73 entries [#1]; H7 remodelling and H3/H8 shortening by one
   turn with a bulky ligand [#2, #5]; the H11-H12 loop and a helix-3 hydrogen-bond pattern remodelled by the
   Y537S construct mutation [#4, #16]. Associations named in those texts: ligand chemistry/size, construct
   mutation, and (in the apo state) intrinsic H3 dynamics. No opened source attributes a non-H12 difference to
   the coactivator peptide.
2. **Missing H12 density is reported per structure in prose and by omission from the deposited model** [#11];
   mobility of a modelled H12 is reported as position-wise main-chain B-factors normalised to the structure
   average [#12]. Interpretation in the ERα literature is split between disorder of the unliganded/undocked
   helix [#13, #14] and crystal-contact influence at one monomer but not the other, with the contaminated chain
   excluded from analysis [#12]. PDB-wide, missing strings are usually far from packing contacts, except at
   chain termini [#10].
3. **Y537S** forms a new hydrogen bond from Ser537 (to D351 in the apo mutant [#16], to E380 in a second series
   [#18]), remodels the H11-H12 loop and buries Leu536 in essentially all deposited Y537S monomers [#4, #17];
   it was introduced as a crystallisation aid because it raises the folded fraction [#4]. **D538G** removes the
   Y537-N348 bond, shifts the start of the H12 helix from 537 to 539, displaces the H11-H12 loop toward H11 and
   increases its flexibility in MD [#16], and relaxes the "spring force" of that loop [#17]. **L536S** is an
   engineered conformation-driving construct mutation [#20]; the nearest opened mechanistic evidence for a
   Leu536 substitution is MD of L536Q, which loses the hydrophobic packing at 536 and reduces helical content
   in H11/H12/L11-12 [#19].
4. **Yes for both.** Residues 331-340 (the omega loop between H2 and H3) and 462-463 (loop after H8/H9 in that
   paper's numbering) are unmodelled in a deposited ERα LBD structure, with very weak density [#22]; the
   331-340 loop is coupled to H3 motion [#23] and the ~460-470 loop is part of the dimer interface [#24].

## Gaps

- **L536S mechanism.** No peer-reviewed source giving the residue-level effect of L536S on H12 or the H11-H12
  loop could be opened (searched with and without the co-mutations L372S/L372R and the Cys→Ser set). This
  matters for us: 243 of 788 chains carry L536S. What is established from opened text is only that it is an
  engineered conformation-driving construct mutation [#20] and that another Leu536 substitution (L536Q) loses
  the hydrophobic packing at 536 in MD [#19].
- **Coactivator peptide.** No opened source attributes a *non-H12* structural difference to the bound peptide,
  which SV07 finds for 331 and 466-468 and SV11 for the AF-2 groove pocket. Our result stands without
  literature support on this point.
- **Residue 467 specifically.** The opened evidence names 462-463 as unmodelled and the H8-H9 loop as part of
  the dimer interface; no opened source discusses 464-468 as such.
