# Literature checked for the 28 Oct and 4 Nov questions

Every point below comes from the full text or abstract of the cited source. Paywalled primary sources that could not be
read are listed under Gaps; numbers quoted second-hand are marked as such.

## Is H12 the only part of the ERα LBD that differs between crystal structures?

No. Published comparisons also report:

- **H11 moves with ligand chemistry**, down to one methyl group; Leu525 and His524 change rotamer (Nettles et al. 2008,
  Nat Chem Biol 4:241, doi 10.1038/nchembio.76). A survey of 73 structures sorts them into three His524 classes, with an
  expanded pocket for ligands longer than 13 Å (Lee & Barron 2017, PLOS ONE 12:e0169607, doi 10.1371/journal.pone.0169607).
- **H3, H7 and H8**: a bulky tagged estradiol shortens H3 and H8 by one turn and disrupts H7 (Li et al. 2011,
  J Med Chem 54:3575, doi 10.1021/jm200192y); in the apo state H3 acts as a lever that distorts H1, the H1-H3 loop, H6 and
  H7 (McDougal et al. 2025, bioRxiv, doi 10.1101/2025.02.09.637324).
- **Loops 331-340 and 462-463 are left unmodelled** for very weak density in a deposited structure (PDB 2YAT; Li 2011):
  the omega loop between H2 and H3, and the H9-H10 loop. 331-340 follows H3 motion (McDougal 2025) and the 460-470
  region belongs to the dimer interface (Tanenbaum et al. 1998, PNAS 95:5998, doi 10.1073/pnas.95.11.5998). These are the two loops where our groups differ
  outside the lid.
- **Construct mutations act beyond H12**: Y537S adds a Ser537 hydrogen bond to D351, remodels the H11-H12 loop and
  buries Leu536 in essentially every Y537S monomer, and was introduced because it raises the folded fraction
  (Nettles 2008); a second series finds the bond to E380 (Hosfield et al. 2022, eLife 11:e72512,
  doi 10.7554/eLife.72512). D538G moves the start of the H12 helix from 537 to 539 and loosens the H11-H12 loop
  (Fanning et al. 2016, eLife 5:e12792, doi 10.7554/eLife.12792); both mutations relax a "spring" in that loop
  (Mayne et al. 2021, Mol Cancer Res 19:1559, doi 10.1158/1541-7786.MCR-20-1017). L536S is an engineered
  conformation-driving mutation that made crystallisation possible (PDB 2QXS; Bruning et al. 2010, doi 10.1038/nchembio.451).
- **Missing H12 density** is reported per structure in prose and by omission from the model (Fanning 2016); H12
  mobility as normalised main-chain B-factors, with crystal contacts named as influencing H12 "of one monomer but not
  the other" (Hosfield 2022). PDB-wide, missing strings sit in loops and away from packing contacts, except at chain ends
  (Djinovic-Carugo & Carugo 2015, Intrinsically Disord Proteins 3:e1095697, doi 10.1080/21690707.2015.1095697).
- No wild-type apo ERα LBD crystal structure exists (Mayne 2021); the apo state was solved from a fish orthologue
  (McDougal 2025).

## Do VDR superagonists change H12?

Not its position. Three superagonist families keep H12 "strictly maintained", Cα RMSD 0.08-0.38 Å against the reference
complex (Tocchini-Valentini et al. 2001, PNAS 98:5491, doi 10.1073/pnas.091018698; Eelen et al. 2005, Mol Pharmacol
67:1566, doi 10.1124/mol.104.008730). Superagonism is explained by a longer-lived active complex, stronger coactivator
binding and extra ligand contacts with H12 residues (Eelen et al. 2008, Chem Biol 15:1029,
doi 10.1016/j.chembiol.2008.08.008), so "same H12 position" is supported and "no H12 difference at all" is not.
In VDR, H12 moves with antagonists and partial agonists, not between agonist subtypes (Yamamoto 2019, Chem Pharm Bull
67:609, doi 10.1248/cpb.c19-00131).

## Do SERDs destabilise H12 more than SERMs, and is that why the receptor is degraded?

Destabilisation is reported mainly in solution: fulvestrant raises deuterium uptake near H12 while E2 protects it
(Fanning et al. 2018, eLife 7:e37161, doi 10.7554/eLife.37161); limited proteolysis ranks apo 2.2 ≈ fulvestrant 2.3 <
OP-1074 3.5 < 4-OHT 6.0 min (Fanning et al. 2018, Nat Commun 9:2368, doi 10.1038/s41467-018-04413-3). In crystals H12 of
SERD complexes stays in the AF-2 cleft, with higher B-factors, a disordered H11-H12 loop and the helix starting at 536
(Diennet et al. 2023, bioRxiv, doi 10.1101/2023.10.31.564979). A panel study finds SERM and SERD structures "very similar"
and receptor loss possibly unnecessary for antagonism (Min et al. 2021, PNAS 118:e2101657118, doi 10.1073/pnas.2101657118).
The step from H12 destabilisation to degradation is stated as unresolved (Nat Commun 2018). The one structure with H12
fully destabilised is rat ERβ with ICI164,384 (PDB 1HJ1; Pike et al. 2001, Structure 9:145).

## What pocket and ligand volumes are published for ERα?

About 450 Å³ for the pocket and about 250 Å³ for E2 or DES, quoted second-hand from the 1997 estradiol structure paper
without a method (Tanenbaum 1998; Katzenellenbogen 2011, J Med Chem 54:5271, doi 10.1021/jm200801h). A bulky substituent
enlarges the pocket by 40 % (Nettles et al. 2007, EMBO Rep 8:563, doi 10.1038/sj.embor.7400963). Only VDR papers report
pocket, ligand and occupancy together (58-63 %). Our grid-based volumes therefore cannot be compared like for like; the
lower mode of our E2 pocket (505-579 Å³) is close to the published 450 Å³.
